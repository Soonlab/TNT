## 2. 3대 핵심 발견 (현재 상태)

### Narrative 1 — Pre-CRT tumor-intrinsic predictor
- Hallmark GSEA: E2F targets NES=2.78, G2M, MYC, DSB/HDR ↑ in good (P < 10⁻¹⁰), EMT ↓
- **Nested outer-LOOCV LASSO AUC = 0.650 [0.45, 0.83]**; ElasticNet 0.686 [0.49, 0.85]
- Non-nested (leaked) AUC 0.755 병기 → 투명성
- 95% CI가 0.5 터치 → discovery-stage, TNT-matched validation 대기
- Fig 4 (GSEA) + Fig 5 (ML predictor)

### Narrative 2 — Radiation-induced cascade (exploratory, n=14 paired)
- 5-step cascade: mutation clearance → neoantigen clearance → HLA-LOH clone elimination → Treg/MHC-II/CD8 exhaustion reprogramming → B-cell infiltration
- BCa 5000× bootstrap CIs:
  - **Treg Δ +1.21 [+0.06, +1.97]** — robust, MW P=0.026 (사이그룹 CI 0 제외)
  - 나머지 (MHC_II, CD8_exhaustion, IGH, SBS5, neo binders, neo sites, TRB Shannon) — within-good CI는 robust하나 사이그룹 CI가 0을 포함 → **exploratory**
- HLA-LOH 엄격기준 (Bonferroni corrected): subj 3 (2→0) + subj 4 (1→0) 두 명, 모두 good에서 완전 해소
- Fig 6 (cascade BCa forest) + Fig 8 (HLA/neoantigen) + Fig 9 (clonal)

### Narrative 3 — External CD8-cytotoxic reproducibility (>1,000 patients / 10 cohorts)
- **9-cohort Stouffer meta Z = +2.74, P = 0.006** (N=721, 8/9 정방향)
- Akiyoshi et al 2023 (GSE216616, n=298): cytotoxic lymphocyte OR=3.81 [1.82, 7.97], GZMA×PRF1 P=0.005, IFN-γ Hallmark enriched
- 통합 총 evidence > 1,000 patients / 10 independent cohorts
- 전제: pure CD8_cytotoxic 시그니처 (CD8A/B, GZMA/B/H/K, PRF1, IFNG, NKG7, GNLY, CXCL9/10/11, TBX21, EOMES, KLRK1, KLRD1) — 종양 proliferation 유전자 제외
- v1 classifier bug ("Non-responder" substring misclassify) + CD8_proliferation cell-cycle 혼입 수정
- Fig 7

- **Cascade BCa**: 5,000 resamples, BCa α=0.05
