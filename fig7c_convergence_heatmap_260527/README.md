**Visual motif applied here.** Because the underlying result is a strict null
(0/36 partial P < 0.05; 0/36 BH q < 0.05), I followed the Mariathasan / Thorsson
convention of (a) clipping the color scale at ±0.5 — a value range that lets the
strongest observed |r| (≈0.48) reach saturation while keeping the bulk of the
grid near-white — and (b) avoiding any text or symbol that could be misread as
a positive finding. The figure is dominated by pale cells; the one editorial
emphasis is a gold border around the headline pair (DSB × Δ CD8-cytotoxic), so
the reader can locate the manuscript-quoted pair at a glance without conflating
it with significance.

## 3. Sanity check — headline pair

Headline pair: **DSB repair × Δ CD8-cytotoxic** (manuscript §3.9).

| Framework | r | P | Matches manuscript? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Plain Spearman** | **−0.070** | **0.829** | ✅ exact match to manuscript text "r = −0.07, P = 0.83" |
| Partial Spearman, response-adjusted | −0.168 | 0.601 | matches `targeted_convergence_test.tsv` (`260418_add/`) |

Note: the manuscript text quotes the plain Spearman value, while the test
machinery is partial Spearman. Both are null at this pair, and both yield
0/36 BH q < 0.05 across the full grid. **The cell shown in Fig 7C displays the
partial r (−0.17), matching the figure's stated method (partial Spearman,
response-group adjusted, BH-corrected).** The figure caption should state the
method as "partial Spearman ρ adjusted for response group, BH-corrected across
36 pairs"; the manuscript headline-pair sentence can keep "r = −0.07, P = 0.83"
or switch to "partial r = −0.17, P = 0.60" — both are internally consistent.

## 5. Color scale

- **Palette**: two-arm diverging ramp anchored at 0
  - negative arm: white (255, 255, 255) → ColorBrewer RdBu blue (33, 102, 172)
  - positive arm: white (255, 255, 255) → ColorBrewer RdBu red (178, 24, 43)
- **Limits**: clipped at **[−0.5, +0.5]**
- **Rationale**: the largest observed |partial r| is 0.48, so ±0.5 lets the
  strongest cells reach saturation without exaggerating the null. Wider limits
  (e.g. ±1) would wash every cell into uniform off-white.
- **Cell text color**: white when |r| ≥ 0.40 (only the 4 darkest cells),
  black otherwise — maintains WCAG contrast against the cell fill.
- **Headline pair**: gold (218, 165, 32) border at 2.5 pt; all other cells
  have a 0.5 pt light-grey (200, 200, 200) border.
